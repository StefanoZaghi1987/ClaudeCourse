## Writing Style and Tone

- **Professional yet accessible**: Clear, precise Italian appropriate for business/technical training
- **Instructive**: Written to teach the trainer, who will then teach participants
- **Specific and actionable**: Concrete examples and instructions, not vague generalities
- **Comprehensive**: Thorough coverage without unnecessary verbosity
- **Structured**: Clear hierarchy with headers, bullet points, numbered lists where appropriate
- **Engaging**: Use of callout boxes, tips, warnings, and examples to maintain interest

### Formatting Guidelines

Use these Markdown elements (which will be preserved in the .docx output):

- **Headers**: # for chapter, ## for main topics, ### for subtopics
- **Bold** for key terms and emphasis
- **Italic** for subtle emphasis and foreign terms
- **Numbered lists** for sequential steps
- **Bullet points** for non-sequential items
- **Code blocks** for technical content when relevant
- **Callout boxes** (use blockquotes with emoji):
  - 💡 **Suggerimento**: for helpful tips
  - ⚠️ **Attenzione**: for warnings and common pitfalls
  - ✅ **Best Practice**: for recommended approaches
  - 🎯 **Obiettivo Didattico**: for learning objectives
  - 🔍 **Esempio**: for practical examples
  - 🛠️ **Esercizio**: for hands-on activities
  - 💬 **Domanda di Discussione**: for engagement questions
